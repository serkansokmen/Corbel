# Tidewater visitor privacy notice

This notice explains what the fictional Tidewater installation records about visitors in the main gallery of Harbour Hall.

## What the sensors see

Tidewater uses two depth sensors mounted above the LED wall. Depth sensors measure distance, not colour, so they produce silhouettes rather than photographs. The installation cannot recognise faces and does not attempt to identify anyone.

No conventional video cameras are installed as part of Tidewater. The venue's own security cameras are covered by the venue's separate privacy policy.

## What is stored

The installation keeps an anonymous count of how many people interacted with the wall in each hour, for the venue's visitor reports. These hourly counts contain no images and are kept for 13 months.

Silhouette frames are held in memory only while they are used to drive the visuals. A short silhouette clip from the most recent interaction can be saved for fault diagnosis. Diagnostic clips are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

## Sharing

Hourly counts are shared with Harbour Hall each month. Diagnostic clips never leave the media server, except when the integrator downloads one over the maintenance VPN to investigate a reported fault.

Nothing recorded by Tidewater is sold or used for advertising.

## Signage and choice

A sign at each gallery entrance explains that depth sensors are in use. Visitors who prefer not to be sensed can view the installation from the balcony, which is outside the sensors' range.

## Questions

Questions about this notice can be sent to privacy@harbourhall.example. The venue answers privacy requests within 30 days.
