# Tidewater change log

Changes to the fictional Tidewater installation after its public opening. Newest entries are at the bottom.

## Version 1.1, 14 March

The ambient sequence now starts with a slow fade instead of a cut, after visitor feedback that the cut felt like a crash.

Sensor tracking was retuned to ignore objects shorter than 90 cm, so that pushchairs and bags left on the floor no longer trigger the visuals.

## Version 1.2, 2 April

Added the Restart Sensors button to the staff tablet. Before this version, restarting the sensors required restarting the whole show.

The hourly interaction count now excludes the first 10 minutes after opening, when staff walk through the gallery.

## Version 1.3, 19 May

Installed a second fibre link between the media server and the LED processor as a backup. If the main link fails, the processor switches to the backup link in under a second.

The tablet app now shows a warning when the media server's disk is more than 85 percent full.

## Version 1.4, 30 June

Replaced amplifier AMP-2 under warranty after it entered protect mode repeatedly during hot weather. A fan was added to the rack door.

Added a Large Text mode to the tablet app for staff who find the status screen hard to read.
