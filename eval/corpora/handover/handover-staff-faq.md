# Tidewater questions from gallery staff

Plain-language answers to questions that front-of-house staff ask about the fictional Tidewater installation.

## Can visitors touch the wall?

No. The wall reacts to movement in front of it, so there is no need to touch it. Ask visitors to stay behind the floor line, which is 1 metre from the wall.

## Is it safe for children?

Yes. The wall surface stays below 40 degrees Celsius, and the sound level is capped. Children under 8 should be accompanied by an adult in the gallery.

## A visitor asks if they are being filmed. What do I say?

Explain that the sensors see shapes, like shadows, and do not record faces. Point them to the sign at the entrance and to the privacy notice at the front desk.

## Who do I call when something breaks?

Call the integrator's support line, which responds to fault reports within one business day. Write down the time the fault started before you call.

## Can we turn the installation off for a private event?

Yes. Use the Shutdown button on the tablet as you would at closing time. If the event needs the installation running after normal hours, tell the operations manager so the integrator can adjust the schedule.

## Can we play our own music through the speakers?

No. The sound system is tuned for Tidewater only and the tablet has no audio input. Use the venue's portable PA for announcements or event music.

## How many people can use it at once?

The sensors can track up to 12 people at the same time. With more people in front of the wall, the visuals still react, but individual movements become harder to see.
