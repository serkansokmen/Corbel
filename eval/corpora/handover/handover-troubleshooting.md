# Tidewater fault guide

Work through the steps in order. If a step does not fix the problem, report the fault to the integrator with the time it started and what you tried.

## The whole LED wall is black

Check the PDU-A key switch first. If the switch is on and the wall stays black, look at the LED processor on the second shelf of the rack. A red front light on the processor means it has lost its video signal from the media server.

If the processor light is red, restart the media server from the tablet using Restart Show. Do not pull the HDMI or fibre cables at the back of the processor.

## One panel or a strip of the wall is dark

A single dark panel is usually a loose data cable behind the wall. Staff must not open the wall. Leave the show running and report the panel position, counting columns from the left and rows from the bottom.

## No sound

Check that the tablet volume slider is not at zero. If the slider is up and there is still no sound, the amplifier may have entered protect mode after a power dip. Switch amplifier AMP-2 off, wait 30 seconds, and switch it back on.

## Visitors are not affecting the visuals

The depth sensors only track people between 1.5 and 6 metres from the wall. Very dark clothing and strong sunlight from the skylight can also reduce tracking.

If nobody is tracked even at the right distance, open Sensor View on the tablet. Each sensor should show a live silhouette image. A frozen image means the sensor has stopped; use Restart Sensors on the tablet.

## The staff tablet is frozen

Hold the tablet power button for 10 seconds to force a restart. The show keeps running on the media server while the tablet restarts, so visitors will not notice.

If the tablet cannot connect after restarting, check that it is joined to the Wi-Fi network named TW-STAFF. It will not work on the public visitor network.

## Fire alarm

When the fire alarm sounds, the installation blacks out the wall and mutes the sound automatically through the alarm interlock. After the alarm is cleared, the show does not restart by itself; restart it from the tablet.
