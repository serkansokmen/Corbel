# Harbour Hall technical sheet: main gallery

Harbour Hall is a fictional venue. This sheet describes the facilities in the main gallery that are available to visiting installations.

## Power

The main gallery has two dedicated 32 A single-phase circuits for installations, labelled G1 and G2 on the distribution board in the plant room. General sockets on the gallery walls are on a separate 16 A circuit and must not be used for show equipment.

All installation power is cut automatically when the fire alarm is triggered. Equipment must tolerate an unplanned power loss without damage.

## Network

The venue provides a dedicated installation VLAN with no internet access by default. Outbound internet access for remote maintenance is opened on request through a VPN that the venue IT team controls.

The visitor Wi-Fi is a separate network and cannot reach the installation VLAN.

## Building systems

The building management system restarts all installation computers on the installation VLAN at 05:00 every day, as part of the venue's maintenance window.

The gallery skylight blinds close automatically at 11:00 and open again at 15:00 to protect the artworks from direct sun.

## Light and sound limits

Venue policy limits any display in the main gallery to 1000 nits during public opening hours, because of the light-sensitive works in the adjacent room.

The noise limit in the main gallery is 78 dB(A) at the centre of the room.

## Access

Contractors may work in the gallery between 07:00 and 09:30 on weekdays, before the venue opens to the public. Weekend access must be booked with the operations manager at least five working days ahead.

The loading dock door is 2.4 metres wide and 3 metres high. The goods lift to the gallery floor takes loads up to 1500 kg.
