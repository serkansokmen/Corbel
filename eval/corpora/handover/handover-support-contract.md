# Tidewater support and maintenance agreement (summary)

This is a fictional summary of the support agreement between Harbour Hall (the venue) and the integrator that built Tidewater.

## Term

The agreement runs for 24 months from the public opening of the installation. Either party may end it with 90 days' written notice.

## Response times

The integrator responds to every fault report within 4 hours. A fault that blacks out the whole LED wall counts as critical, and for critical faults an engineer attends on site within 1 working day.

Fault reports must be sent to the support line at support@integrator.example or by phone. Reports sent to individual staff members do not start the response clock.

## Preventive maintenance

The integrator visits the gallery every quarter to clean the sensor windows, check the LED wall for dead pixels, and install software updates. Each visit takes place before public opening hours.

## Spare parts

The venue stores a spare parts kit on site: four LED panels, one depth sensor, one tablet and a set of data cables. The integrator replaces any spare that is used within 10 working days.

## Exclusions

Damage caused by visitors, liquids or work by third parties is not covered. Moving any part of the installation without the integrator present also voids cover for that part.

## Data processing annex

The integrator processes visitor data only on the venue's instructions. Diagnostic silhouette clips are retained for no longer than 72 hours and are then deleted from the media server.

Hourly interaction counts are the venue's property. The integrator may use them only to prepare the monthly report.
