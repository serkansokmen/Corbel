# Tidewater daily operation runbook

Tidewater is a fictional interactive installation in the main gallery of Harbour Hall. It consists of an LED wall, a media server, two depth sensors, a four-channel sound system and a staff tablet. This runbook is for venue staff who open and close the installation each day.

## Opening

Switch on the LED wall first, using the key switch on the rack marked PDU-A. Wait until the wall shows the grey calibration grid, which takes about 90 seconds.

Then press the power button on the media server once. The media server boots straight into the show software and fades in the idle scene after roughly two minutes. Do not log in to Windows; the show account signs in automatically.

Open the Tidewater app on the staff tablet and check that the status light at the top is green. A green light means the media server, both depth sensors and the sound processor are all reporting.

## During opening hours

Keep the LED wall at the brightness preset called Gallery. This preset limits the wall to 1200 nits. Never select the Rehearsal preset while visitors are in the room, because it disables the brightness limit.

The sound level in the gallery must stay below 78 dB(A), measured at the bench in the centre of the room. The volume slider on the tablet is capped at this level and cannot be raised further by staff.

The installation runs a 20-minute content cycle: 14 minutes of the interactive tide scene, then a 6-minute ambient sequence in which the sensors are ignored.

## Scheduled restart

The media server restarts itself every day at 06:30 to clear memory. If the gallery opens early for a private event, ask the integrator to move the restart time at least two working days in advance.

## Closing

Use the Shutdown button on the tablet, not the power button on the server. The tablet fades the show to black, stops the sound, then shuts down the media server safely.

Once the media server fan has stopped, turn the PDU-A key switch off to power down the LED wall. Leave the network switch on overnight; it receives remote updates.
